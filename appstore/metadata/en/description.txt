Every day, a short true story from Islamic history, with a lesson to take away. Around it, the everyday tools a Muslim needs, free and usable offline.

THE STORY OF THE DAY
Every user receives the same new story each day: sciences, architecture, Companions, travellers, women scholars, dynasties… Each story ends with a lesson and comes with a reminder: an authentic hadith with its reference (al-Bukhari, Muslim). You can listen to the story or share it as an image.

Daily stories are AI-assisted and fact-checked. Hadiths, duas and adhkar are never AI-generated: they come from recognised collections.
